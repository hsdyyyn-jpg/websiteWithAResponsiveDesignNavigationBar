Section 2 How special fungi helps to solve the plastic pollution

Name Type of plastic to eat
Pestalotiopsis microspora Polyurethane
Pleurotus ostreatus Renewable polyethylene

Since 1960s, more than 8.3 billion tons of plastic have been produced and cause the huge problem of plastic pollution.

In 2011, Yale researchers found that some special fungi could break down plastics and survive in the plastic even with no oxygen. It could work perfectly in landfills and have potential to clean up large ocean areas like pollution areas in the Pacific Ocean. Some fungi even could combine recycling and producing together, like Pleurotus ostreatus growing from plastic garbage and it could be eaten by families.
